Symbols: your 150, wired into CreaBIM's marks

A new Symbols page in Graphic styles. Every kind of mark picks one of its ten styles, kept in the style pack like pens and patterns, so a whole project, or an office's pack, changes look in one place. Ten kinds restyle marks CreaBIM already draws. Five are new, placed with a Symbol tool. Marks still draw with the view's pens and fill their text slot (a grid's letter, a level's height, a tag's number). Every symbol on this page is drawn by the shipped CreaBIM Symbols font.
