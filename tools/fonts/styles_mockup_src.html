Drawing styles: one engine, eight looks

Gemini's seven styles from the two Hand Line Studios, reconciled into one set of 22 settings, plus our Loose. A view picks its drawing style in Properties › Graphics; Adjust this style… opens every setting, grouped as CreaBIM's pens plan files them. Pen weights still come from the pen set, so cut, projection and hatch keep their order in every style. This page draws the footing detail live from those settings, with CreaBIM's own lettering fonts. It is a mockup: the real engine will be Rust, drawing PDF, sheets and screen alike.
