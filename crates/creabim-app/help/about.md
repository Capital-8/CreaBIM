# About CreaBIM

![CreaBIM {version} · build {build}](img/creabim-lockup.png)

**Modelled like Blender, made for architects.**

CreaBIM {version} (build {build}) is an open-source, BIM-native architectural modeller: one model for freeform concept design and permit drawings, with Blender-style modelling, live 2D views and native IFC.

## Philosophy

CreaBIM is design-first BIM. It offers direct modelling for design work, not only documentation:

- Edit mode for points, edges and faces;
- masses with modifiers;
- profiles;
- Divide;
- view areas and workspaces.

You shape the building as freely as in Blender, and it stays a building. The walls, slabs and roofs know what they are, and plans, sections, sheets, schedules and IFC follow from them.
